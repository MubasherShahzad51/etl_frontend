╔════════════════════════════════════╗
    KPI-4. Inventory to Sale Ratio
╚════════════════════════════════════╝

1. What we can do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Metric: Inventory-to-Sales Ratio

2. Why to do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Tells market pressure
► =1  → Balance
► >1  → Oversupply
► <1  → Undersupply

This single KPI explains pricing power.

3. How to do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► From dealer_sales_summary_simple.csv
► Inventory-to-Sales Ratio = SUM(active_inventory) / SUM(total_sales)

4. How we show it?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► KPI number (e.g. 1.06)
► Badge:

 ►🟢 < 0.9  
 ►🟡 0.9–1.1
 ►🔴 > 1.1

5. How LLM uses it?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► With an inventory-to-sales ratio above 1, the market is experiencing oversupply, leading to pricing pressure.

╔════════════════════════════════════╗
       KPI-6. Sales Velocity
╚════════════════════════════════════╝


1. What we can do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Metric: Sales Velocity

2. Why to do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Measures how fast inventory converts into sales
► Separates high-performing dealers from large-inventory dealers
► Explains why some dealers win even with similar inventory levels
► Strong indicator of pricing power and operational efficiency

3. How to do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Dataset: `dealer_sales_summary_simple.csv`
► Columns used:
    • `total_sales`
    • `active_inventory`

► Formula:
Sales Velocity = total_sales / active_inventory
► Calculation scope:
    • Per dealer
    • Market-level average (optional)

4. How we show it?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Displayed inside tables, not as a hero KPI
► Shown as a numeric ratio (e.g. `1.18`)
► Color indicator:
    ► 🟢 High velocity ( > 1.2 )
    ► 🟡 Average ( 0.8 – 1.2 )
    ► 🔴 Slow moving ( < 0.8 )

► Used in:
    • Top Market Leaders table
    • Competitor Intel comparisons
    
5. How LLM uses it?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Top-performing dealers maintain higher sales velocity, converting inventory into sales faster than the market average
► Despite similar inventory levels, this competitor outperforms due to stronger sales velocity.
