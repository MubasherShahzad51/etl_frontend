🍀 INTRODUCTION

Question It Answer will be : "How is the inventory?"
Dashboard should have ONLY 3 jobs:

1. State of the inventory
2. Who is stocked / understocked
3. Where opportunity & risk exist in inventory

Strategic Purpose: Evaluate the health, efficiency, exposure, and structural risk of vehicle supply across dealers and regions. Focus on supply balance, inventory pressure, dealer exposure risk, regional imbalance, brand concentration, and operational efficiency. Core Question: "Is the supply side healthy, efficient, and balanced?"

This differs from the main Dashboard, which focuses on performance and competition ("Who is winning?").

Analytical Limitations: Not possible with current data - Vehicle age analysis, Days on market, Price elasticity, VIN-level turnover, Margin analysis.

╔════════════════════════════════════╗
      KPI-3. Inventory-to-Sales Ratio (Inventory Pressure)
╚════════════════════════════════════╝



1. What we can do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Metric: Inventory-to-Sales Ratio

2. Why to do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Gauges inventory efficiency relative to sales
► >1 indicates potential oversupply risk
► Core oversupply risk metric and supply vs demand balance indicator

3. How to do?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► Based on provided columns
► Columns: active_inventory, total_sales, confirmed_sales, potential_sales
► Inventory-to-Sales Ratio = SUM(active_inventory) / SUM(total_sales) [refine with confirmed_sales + potential_sales]

4. How we show it?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► KPI number (e.g. 1.08)
► Badge: 🟢 <0.9 (Tight), 🟡 0.9–1.1 (Balanced), 🔴 >1.1 (Oversupply)

5. How LLM uses it?
▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃▃

► An inventory-to-sales ratio of 1.08 signals slight oversupply, urging dealers to accelerate turnover.
